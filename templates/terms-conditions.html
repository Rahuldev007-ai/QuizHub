Terms. Honor code.

Governing standards of participation, intellectual integrity, and community conduct across the QuizHub evaluation ecosystem.

Section 01

1. Introduction & Acceptance

Welcome to QuizHub. By accessing and navigating this evaluation platform, you acknowledge and agree to be bound unconditionally by these terms, conditions, and our academic honor code. If you do not align with any portion of these principles, you are advised to refrain from using the platform.

Section 02

2. Scholar Account Integrity

2.1 Creation of an individual scholar account is required to participate in official evaluations and be indexed on the global merit leaderboard.
2.2 You bear singular responsibility for safeguarding your authentication credentials and for all assessment actions recorded under your profile.
2.3 Participants must be at least 13 years of age, or have attained legal digital consent in their jurisdiction.

Section 03

3. Question Content & Intellectual Integrity

3.1 All evaluation materials, test cases, and question formulations are calibrated for academic inquiry.
3.2 The deployment of automated scraping bots, prompt injection tools, or unauthorized session replay scripts is strictly prohibited.
3.3 QuizHub reserves the right to annul verified scores obtained via disingenuous automated means without prior notice.

Section 04

4. Intellectual Property Rights

4.1 All curriculum structures, visual design languages, design tokens, and technical question repositories remain the exclusive intellectual property of QuizHub and its academic contributors.
4.2 Reproduction, redistribution, or mirroring of quiz content for commercial syndication is prohibited without express written authorization.

Section 05

5. Limitation of Liability & Warranty

QuizHub provides this evaluation interface on an "as is" and "as available" basis. While we strive for absolute empirical precision, we make no express warranties regarding uninterrupted availability or error-free scoring.

Section 06

6. Amendments to the Codex

We reserve the authority to update or refine these provisions periodically. Revisions take immediate legal effect upon publication to this URL. Continued participation denotes active acceptance.
